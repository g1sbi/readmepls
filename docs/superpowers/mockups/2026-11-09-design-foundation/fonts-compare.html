The Navier–Stokes equations are partial differential equations which describe the motion of viscous fluid substances. They were named after French engineer and physicist Claude-Louis Navier and the Anglo-Irish physicist and mathematician George Gabriel Stokes. They mathematically express momentum balance for Newtonian fluids.
